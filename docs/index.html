What it is

Endless Keeper is a free, open-source mod of Bullfrog's classic Dungeon Keeper, built on the KeeperFX engine. Everything you love is still here: the imps, the gold, the creatures, the slapping, the delicious evil. What changes is the world around it.

There is no map edge. Every run drops you into a fresh underworld generated from a seed, with biomes, gold seams, rival keepers, hero fortresses and treasure caves as far as your imps can dig. You start small, with a few rooms and spells. Then you explore, find research books, grow your dungeon day by day, and survive what the night brings.

The vision

Dungeon Keeper is one of the most loved strategy games ever made, and there is still nothing quite like it. Endless Keeper is an attempt to bring it back to life by pairing its timeless dungeon building with the gameplay loop that keeps players hooked in today's most popular games:

  • Explore
  • Gather
  • Build
  • Grow stronger
  • Venture further

All of it in an endless world you can share with your friends. Picture Dungeon Keeper meets Minecraft: every run is new, every tunnel can lead somewhere, and your dungeon keeps growing for as long as you keep it alive.

A standalone mod, next to KeeperFX

Endless Keeper installs into its own folder and lives next to KeeperFX: it never changes, replaces or updates a KeeperFX installation, and you can keep and play both.

  • It has its own launcher, and does not use or replace KeeperFX's.
  • Its main menu has no Campaign button, and Continue takes you back to your Endless Mode world.
  • It does not connect to KeeperFX's multiplayer servers.

For Dungeon Keeper's campaign, the community's campaigns or a classic multiplayer match, please get KeeperFX.
