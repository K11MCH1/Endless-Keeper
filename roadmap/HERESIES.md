# Heresies

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Rare tomes, found only far from home, that rewrite one rule of your dungeon at a price. A dungeon can hold two at
most. For example:

- **Open Graves**: no Lairs are needed; creatures sleep where they stand and heal at half speed.
- **Company Scrip**: no wages, but everyone eats twice as much.
- **One Kind**: your portals bring a single kind you name, five levels up, and nothing else.
- **Night Court**: double work by night, and sleep by day.

A tome can be burned at the Temple to undo it.
