# Breeds

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Creatures take after the biome of the portal that brought them. For example:

- A Spider from the Frozen Hollows is a **Frost Spider**, and can Freeze from level 1.
- A Spider from the Ancient Ruins is a **Tomb Spider**, and its bite is poisoned.
- A Beetle from the Volcanic Rifts barely feels fire.
- A Fly from the Flooded Grottos feeds on a little of its victim's blood.
- A Troll from the Sandstone Wastes carries twice as much.

Not every kind has a breed in every biome: only where one makes sense, and never more than one. You can tell a breed
by its tint, and by a word on the creature's panel.

So the make-up of your army depends on which portals you have claimed.
