# The False Heart

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A decoy Dungeon Heart. It is neither a trap nor a door, but the Workshop builds it like one.

A raid's leader heads for the closest Heart he can sense. If a false Heart is closer than your real one, he goes for
that first. So you decide where a raid breaks in: put the decoy beyond your trapped corridor, or behind the wall you
can afford to lose. The trick ends when the decoy is destroyed. The raiders then know, and set off for the real
Heart.

Raiders dig their own way in, so today they seldom walk past your traps and doors. A false Heart leads them there,
and to whatever you have built with [Rigging](RIGGING.md).

## Still being decided

- How strongly a false Heart pulls a raid, what it costs to build, and how often a raid sees through it. It is a
  very powerful tool, and all three have to be balanced.
