# Take Up the Sword

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game. For now it is run with chat commands on a dedicated server: `!sword` followed by the
> other player's name invites them, and `!help` lists the rest.

You can invite another player to lead a raid against your own dungeon. The guest takes over the raid's leader and
plays him in first person. Which wall to break through, when to bring the rest of the party up, whether to make for
the Treasury or the Dungeon Heart: those decisions are now a person's.

- **Switching heroes.** The guest is not tied to the leader. While the raid lasts, they can move from one of its
  heroes into another.
- **Cleverer, not stronger.** A hero with a player inside is exactly as strong as it would have been without one.
- **Only when invited.** A guest joins only at the keeper's invitation, and never while that keeper is away.
- **For the defender:** killing the hero that the guest is driving is worth twice the experience.
- **For the guest:** a tally of the dungeons they have breached. Their own dungeon gains nothing from it.
