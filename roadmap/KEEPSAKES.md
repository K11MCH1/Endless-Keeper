# Keepsakes

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Keepsakes are loot for your creatures. The leaders of raids and the captains of strongholds drop them. Each one is
random, and each changes the stats of the creature that carries it.

- **One each, for creatures with a name.** A creature you have [named](MISDEEDS.md) can carry one keepsake. You give
  it by dropping it on the creature, and from then on it is shown on the creature's panel and beneath its name.
- **It can be lost.** When its bearer dies, the keepsake drops on the spot and goes to whoever picks it up first. On
  a shared world that can be another keeper, though never an ally.
- **Spare keepsakes are stored.** One that no creature carries is kept in the [Storage Room](THE-STORAGE-ROOM.md),
  or in the Dungeon Heart's room if you have none.
