# Alarm Bells

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

## What it is for

Today a hero fortress does nothing more once it has been looted, gold is just as rich wherever you dig it, and
the danger that comes for you follows only the level of your best creature. Alarm Bells make danger follow
wealth instead, and by place: the gold you dig next to the heroes' fortresses is gold they notice.

## How it works

- **Every hero fortress has an alarm bell.** Its reach covers a couple of hundred slabs around the fortress.
- **Mining rings it.** Gold that your imps mine inside a fortress's reach fills your alarm for that fortress.
- **The alarm is yours alone.** Each keeper has their own alarm for each fortress. A neighbour mining the same
  hills fills theirs, not yours.
- **The fortress is as strong as ever.** What it sends depends on how deep it lies, as fortresses do now.

## How the alarm grows

1. **A warning.** A Call to Arms goes up on the seam being mined, with its flag and its horn, and a few heroes
   leave the fortress and walk towards it. The flag is put on your mine rather than on the fortress, which is
   usually still out of sight. This stage costs you nothing but attention: stop mining there, guard the seam,
   or go and raze the fortress.
2. **A raiding party.** Heroes are sent to wreck the Treasury that the mine feeds.
3. **A column.** A full force marches out of the fortress.

## Silencing the bell

Only razing the fortress silences its bell, and then it is silent for good.

## Still being decided

- How far a bell reaches, and how much gold fills each stage.
- Whether heroes answer a Call to Arms. If they don't, the flag and the horn are shown on their own, and the
  heroes are sent to the seam by their party's orders.
- How a hero flag is told apart from a rival keeper's Call to Arms.
