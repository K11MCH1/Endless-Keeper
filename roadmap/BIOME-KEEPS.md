# Biome Keeps

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A hero stronghold is built to suit the ground it stands on:

- **Volcanic Rifts**: the keep stands in lava. One bridge leads in, and Sentry traps cover every step of it.
- **Frozen Hollows**: the keep is carved out of ice, and none of its corridors is wider than a single slab.
- **Flooded Grottos**: the keep is a circle of islands.
- **Sandstone Wastes**: the keep is a tomb. A passage of rolling boulders ends at a sealed chamber.
- **Crystal Caverns**: the keep is a tower without a single door, defended by Lightning traps in great numbers.
- **Ancient Ruins**: the keep is a half-collapsed maze. It can be entered three ways, and the front gate is not one
  of them.

Strongholds in the Earthen Caverns and the Rocky Depths stay as they are today, and so does any stronghold that
already stands in your world.

No single way of breaking in works everywhere any more.
