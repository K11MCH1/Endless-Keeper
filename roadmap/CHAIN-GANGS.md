# Chain Gangs

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

One more fate for a captive, beside those in [A Prisoner's Fate](A-PRISONERS-FATE.md): put it to work, unconverted.

Mark a captive for the gang and it is led out of the Prison in irons to work for you. A Tunneller digs and mines
twice as fast as an imp. Heroes of other kinds carry things.

- **A gang has to be fed.**
- **A gang has to be watched.** It works only while a creature of yours stands guard where it can see the gang.
  Without a guard it slacks.
- **Some get loose.** A captive that gets out of its irons makes for the nearest land the heroes hold.
- **The Prison sets the size.** The bigger your Prison, the bigger the gang it can send out.
