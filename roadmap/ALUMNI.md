# Alumni

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Every portal remembers the creatures it has brought you, and how far they have risen since. Each of them adds to the
portal's renown when it reaches level 10, again at level 25 and again at level 50.

Renown does two things:

- **Newcomers arrive at a higher level.** A portal with forty veterans to its name might send recruits who are
  already level 8.
- **Newcomers arrive sooner.** The wait between arrivals gets shorter.

A creature counts only while it still serves you. And however renowned a portal is, its newcomers always arrive some
levels beneath your best creature.

A newcomer at level 1 is a slow start in a dungeon of veterans. With Alumni, the dungeon's own record shortens it.
