# Hostage Pacts

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Two keepers whose dungeons are within reach of each other can make a pact, signed at their Dungeon Hearts. There are
three kinds:

- **Truce.** Neither side harms the other's Heart or rooms.
- **Open border.** The two keepers' creatures walk past each other and do not fight.
- **Alliance.** Each sees what the other sees, as the allies of a co-op party do.

Hostages seal every pact. The two keepers exchange creatures of level 10 or higher, and each holds the other's
creature in their Prison, well fed and untouched, for as long as the pact lasts. Whoever breaks the pact leaves
their hostage to the other's Torture Chamber.

Most keepers live too far apart to need one. A pact is for keepers who happen to live close together, and for worlds
where keepers go to war, as in [Portal Wars](PORTAL-WARS.md).
