# Backfill and Masonry

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Imps learn to put ground back, with two new orders:

- **Backfill.** You mark open floor, and the imps fill it in with earth. Filled earth is ordinary earth, so it can
  be reinforced.
- **Masonry.** On your own floor, the imps build a wall where you ask for one. Every slab of wall costs gold.

Between them you can mend a wall the heroes broke through, turn a crooked cave into a proper corridor, shut a
raiders' tunnel for good, or lay out a maze that digging alone would never give you.

Neither order works on another keeper's land or right beside it, and neither can be used to wall in another keeper's
creature.
