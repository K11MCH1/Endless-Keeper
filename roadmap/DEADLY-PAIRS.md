# Deadly Pairs

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game.

A creature with two [vices](MISDEEDS.md) can have them melted down into one sin: a new trait with its own name and
its own effect.

It is done on the rack in the Torture Chamber. Both vices are used up, and a creature can carry only one sin, so it
can be done once for each creature.

One pair, as an example: Do-Gooder's Bane and Afraid of Nothing in the Dark become the **Night Butcher**, who is
healed by every hero it kills after dusk.

The other pairs are not listed anywhere. They are there to be found.
