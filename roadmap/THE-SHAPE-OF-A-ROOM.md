# The Shape of a Room

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A well-enclosed room already works better. Now the way a room is cut gives it a character as well. For example:

- **Lair.** Small cells stop creatures that hate each other from brawling over their beds. One great hall heals its
  sleepers faster when it is full.
- **Training Room.** A long, narrow room is a gallery, the better school for creatures that shoot. A square room is
  a ring, the better school for creatures that fight hand to hand.
- **Library.** Dead-end alcoves speed up [mastery](AMENITY-MASTERY.md) research. An open floor speeds up the
  learning of new things.
- **Treasury.** With a single door it is a vault, and each of its slabs holds more gold.

A room's flag names its shape as soon as the room qualifies.

[Neighbours](NEIGHBOURS.md) rewards what a room is built next to. This rewards how the room itself is laid out.
