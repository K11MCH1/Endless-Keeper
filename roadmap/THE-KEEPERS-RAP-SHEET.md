# The Keeper's Rap Sheet

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The keeper gets a record like their creatures' [Rap Sheet](MISDEEDS.md): a page of tracked feats written in the
game's voice, each in five tiers, each tier paying player experience. For example:

- **Poultry in motion**: heroes turned into chickens.
- **Bone idle**: Skeletons raised in the Prison.
- **Frequent flyer**: creatures dropped into battle.
- **Night shift**: an outpost cleared between dusk and dawn.
- **Clean hands**: a raid beaten without casting a spell.

Your top three feats show beside your name in the player panel.
