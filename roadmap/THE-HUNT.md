# The Hunt

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Every wild kind gets a ladder of ranks, and you climb it by hunting that kind at night.

- **The call.** When you have killed enough of one kind after dusk (Spiders, say), its **Alpha** comes looking for
  you the following night.
- **The Alpha** stands two levels above its pack and has one trick of its own. It comes up out of the nearest burrow
  and walks to your dungeon, and it may dig for your Dungeon Heart as far as your walls.
- **The reward.** Each Alpha you kill raises your rank with its kind.

There are five ranks for each kind. The early ones make its dead drop more gold, the later ones thin out its packs
near your Heart, and the last one brings a tame creature of that kind to your Lair.
