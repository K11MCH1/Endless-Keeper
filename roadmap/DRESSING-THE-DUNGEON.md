# Dressing the Dungeon

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A dungeon can wear the look of another biome. Each biome has walls and floors of its own, and you unlock a biome's
look for your own dungeon by what you do there: destroy ten strongholds in the Frozen Hollows, for example, and your
corridors can be winter stone.

Some looks belong to no biome at all. Those are given for [feats](THE-KEEPERS-RAP-SHEET.md) and
[anniversaries](ANNIVERSARIES-OF-THE-HEART.md).

- **A switch** turns the look on or off.
- **It is only for show.** A look makes no difference to how the dungeon works, and visitors see it too.
