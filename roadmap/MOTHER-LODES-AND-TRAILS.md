# Mother Lodes and Trails

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

At present there is as much gold beside your dungeon as there is a thousand slabs away. That changes: gold near home
becomes scarcer, and what there is points the way to more.

- **Trails.** A seam is no longer one lump. It is a string of small ones, each a short dig from the last, stretching
  for a hundred slabs or so.
- **Mother lodes.** At the far end of a trail is its mother lode: a solid mass of gold as big as several seams
  together. Something has made its home there.
- **Richer by circle.** The farther out the [circle](CIRCLES-OF-HELL.md), the richer its lodes.

Every start has a trail of its own, and a trail can always be followed to its end.

Mining turns from digging out the nearest lump into following the gold.

Mother lodes and trails change the ground itself, so they arrive in newly created worlds; worlds that already exist
keep their ground.
