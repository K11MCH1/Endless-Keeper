# Past Lives

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game.

An old veteran can start again as a new creature. Give a creature of level 30 or higher to the pool of the Temple,
and its soul returns in the next creature your portals send, of whatever kind that happens to be. The new creature
carries over three things:

- **The name** of the creature it was, with a note about that earlier life.
- **One [vice](MISDEEDS.md)** of the old creature, picked at random.
- **One ability** of its old kind, which its new kind could never have learnt. An Orc might still know the Fireball
  it threw as a Warlock.

Every past life shows on the creature. For each one, a petal of its health flower becomes a triangle.
