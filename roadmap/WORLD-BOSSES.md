# World Bosses

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game.

Once every 24 hours a World Boss can be fought. Players sign up for the fight, send their best creatures to a small
world apart from their own, and bring the boss down with nothing but those creatures and their spells.

## Joining

- **The timer.** A new part of the interface counts down to the next World Boss.
- **The button.** When the fight is ready, a button to join appears there.
- **Your team.** The button brings up a window where you pick the best of your creatures for the fight.

## The fight

- **A world apart.** At the start of the round, everyone who joined is moved to another world, no bigger than the
  map of the original campaign's first level. The creatures they picked are teleported in.
- **No Hand.** You cannot pick your creatures up there. Spells are the only tools you have.
- **The boss.** A World Boss is a creature of the game at three to four times its usual size. The Avatar is one
  example.
- **The pull.** The World Boss draws your creatures the way a Dungeon Heart draws heroes, and they attack it.
- **Slow and heavy.** It strikes slowly, but each blow is terrible, and it has an enormous amount of health.
- **Real losses.** A creature that dies in the fight is gone for good, unless some other feature on this roadmap
  rules otherwise.

## Your dungeon meanwhile

Your dungeon does not carry on without you. For the length of the fight it is paused, as it would be if you had gone
offline.

## The loot

- **What it leaves.** When the World Boss falls, it leaves gold, books and loot behind.
- **Your own.** Every player has a drop of their own, which only they can see and only they can take.
- **Thirty seconds.** From the moment the boss dies you have 30 seconds to gather your loot. Then everyone is
  returned to their own dungeon.
- **Home with you.** Whatever you gathered is waiting in your [Storage Room](THE-STORAGE-ROOM.md) when you get back,
  or in the room of your Dungeon Heart if you have not built one.

World Bosses can be combined with other updates, [Misdeeds](MISDEEDS.md) and its vices among them. How they tie in
is still open.
