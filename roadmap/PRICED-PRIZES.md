# Priced Prizes

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The best finds in the world stop being free. Gems, the richest caves and neutral portals are each placed with
something in the way. For example:

- **Lava** between you and the prize.
- **A ring of rock** all the way round it, with one plug of gold as the only way through. Dig out the gold and the
  way is open, in as well as out.
- **A fortress's bell** hanging over it (see [Alarm Bells](ALARM-BELLS.md)).
- **A nest** of wild creatures in front of it.
- **Distance**: the prize is a long way from any path.

You can see the prize on the map. What stands between you and it, you find out on the way. Near a start, prices stay
low.

Priced Prizes change the ground itself, so they arrive in newly created worlds; worlds that already exist keep their
ground.
