# Defectors

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game.

When an angry creature walks out on you, it no longer just disappears. It leaves by your portal. If some other
keeper on the same server can offer what you did not (the room it wanted, the wages it was owed or a lair of its
own), it comes out of one of their portals.

- **It brings its past.** A defector keeps its level, its name and its [Rap Sheet](MISDEEDS.md), and carries a line
  naming the keeper it left.
- **It is no giant.** Its level on arrival is never higher than the best creature its new keeper already has.
- **It has a reason.** Nothing defects without cause, and the cause is written on its Rap Sheet. Nor can one keeper
  stir up another keeper's creatures from a distance.

A well-run dungeon ends up with its neighbours' best creatures. A badly run one supplies them.
