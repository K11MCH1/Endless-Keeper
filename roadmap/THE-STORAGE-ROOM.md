# The Storage Room

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A new room for loot. It keeps the [Keepsakes](KEEPSAKES.md) that none of your creatures is carrying, and whatever
you bring home from a [World Boss](WORLD-BOSSES.md). A dungeon without a Storage Room keeps its loot in the room of
the Dungeon Heart.

## Still being decided

- How large it is, and how much it holds.
- What else it may hold.
- Whether raiders can rob it.
