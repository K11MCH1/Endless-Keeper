# Threats

> [!NOTE]
> These are planned features. They are not in the game yet, and details may change before they arrive.

## What it is for

Today danger comes for you in one shape: a raid sized by your best creature. These ideas give the world more ways to
push back, so that going further, getting richer and leaving things unfinished each carry their own risk, and so
that losing a fight can cost you something without ending your dungeon.

## Circle laws

From the second [circle](CIRCLES-OF-HELL.md) outward, each circle adds one rule of its own besides stronger
enemies, revealed when you first enter it. For example:

- Imps will not dig at night outside claimed land.
- Portals there bring nothing until that region's fortress falls.
- Creatures eat twice as often.
- Nothing heals outside a Lair.

Going further means playing differently, not only fighting harder. A circle is measured from the nearest start,
so a law applies to a place, and two neighbours digging there live under the same one.

## Consecrated ground

A hero Monk, or a chapel inside an outpost, sheds a small ring where your Hand does nothing: no drops, no pick-ups,
no slaps and no spells, until the Monk dies or the altar is broken. A raid that brings one is beaten by your layout,
your doors, your traps and the parties you send on foot, not by dropping a crowd of creatures on its head. The ring
is small and drawn plainly on the ground.

## The Crusade road

A hero fortress you have disturbed and left standing founds a new outpost one region nearer to you every few days,
up to three. Outposts on the same road answer each other's alarms. Raze the fortress and its road dies with it.
A road never grows towards a keeper who is away or still new, and never closer than 100 slabs to a Heart.

## Rivals with ambitions

A rival keeper you wake no longer dozes off again after a while. It takes an ambition, shown on its map marker:

- **The Hoarder** tunnels towards your far Treasuries.
- **The Portal-grabber** claims the neutral portals near your home, each one a portal's worth of creatures you
  will not have.
- **The Warlord** trains behind closed doors on a countdown you can see, and then comes with everything.

An ambition is aimed at the keeper who woke the rival, never at a bystander.

## Scars

Losing a fight without losing the dungeon:

- A room wrecked by raiders is **desecrated**: it works at half efficiency until your imps relay it and a creature
  prays over it.
- A Heart beaten below a quarter of its health keeps a **crack** that takes a tenth off its health, until you mend it
  with a hero's heart-shard or a heavy sum of gold.

A dungeon carries at most two scars, and a scar never takes it below four fifths of what it was.

## The Ledger of Dread

Raids are sized from a budget instead of from your single best creature: the levels of your whole army, the gold you
hold, the defences you have standing, and how the last raids went. A keeper who has just lost most of an army gets
a floor of mercy. The budget shows as a skull meter by the dial, so hiring a Dragon or filling the Treasury is felt
before the next raid lands.
