# Virtues

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The mirror of [vices](MISDEEDS.md), and in this game a virtue is a flaw. For example:

- A creature whose record shows running away earns **Prudence**, and runs sooner.
- One that slept through raids earns **Temperance**, and trains more slowly.
- One that only ever knocks enemies out earns **Mercy**, and will not finish them.

A spell on the rack corrects one virtue, at the cost of the creature's work for a day.
