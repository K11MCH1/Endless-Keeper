# Move the Heart

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

When the land round a dungeon has nothing left to give, the dungeon can pack up and go.

1. **Order the Exodus** at the Dungeon Heart. The Heart has to be above three quarters of its health.
2. **The Heart shrinks to an ember.** Eight imps pick it up, your creatures gather round them, and the whole dungeon
   sets off on foot.
3. **On the road** there is no place to heal and no wall to stand behind, and raids still come. The ember can take
   as much punishment as the Heart could. If it dies, everything ends with it.
4. **Choose the new home.** Put the ember down on open ground, far from where you started, and a new keep rises
   round it. What you left behind stands as ruins.

On a shared world, no keeper can strike another keeper's ember unless the server lets keepers fight each other.
