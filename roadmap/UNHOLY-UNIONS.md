# Unholy Unions

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Two amenities at their top [mastery](AMENITY-MASTERY.md) can be joined at the Temple into one named union with a
new behaviour. For example:

- **Library and Sight of Evil**: a Scrying Room that always sees around itself.
- **Prison and the Lightning trap**: the Chair, which makes captives talk.
- **Hatchery and Poison Gas**: chickens that poison the heroes who steal them.
- **Lair and Heal**: an Infirmary.

Each room can take one union, chosen from two.
