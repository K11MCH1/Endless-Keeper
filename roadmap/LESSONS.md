# Lessons

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Once a creature has a name, you choose how it grows. Every tenth level it is offered a choice:

- **Learn** the ability its kind would normally get next.
- **Master** an ability it already knows, which makes it stronger and quicker to use again. The new ability is put
  off by ten levels.

By level 60, one Warlock knows six spells and another knows three that hit far harder.

The choice is offered on the creature's [Rap Sheet](MISDEEDS.md), in the same way as the pick of a vice. Creatures
without a name learn in the usual order, so you are asked only about the ones you care about.
