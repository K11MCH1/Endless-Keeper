# Lie of the Land

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The biome under a room changes what the room can do. For example:

- **Volcanic Rifts**: a Workshop becomes a forge, and its work goes faster.
- **Frozen Hollows**: a Graveyard preserves a body for twice as long.
- **Ancient Ruins**: a Library is quicker at [mastery](AMENITY-MASTERY.md) research.
- **Crystal Caverns**: a Treasury holds more gold on each slab.
- **Flooded Grottos**: a Hatchery beside the water hatches more chickens.
- **Sandstone Wastes**: a Training Room is an arena.

A dungeon stands in one biome. To get another biome's bonus, you build an outlying room there, and hold it. That
gives the other biomes a use beyond being crossed: you settle in them.
