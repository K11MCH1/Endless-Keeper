# The Hand Grows

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game.

Player levels pay out on the Hand of Evil itself:

- **A bigger grip.** The Hand holds more creatures as you level: nine, then ten, and on up to sixteen.
- **A wider slap.** A slap also hurries the creatures standing next to the one you slap.

The Hand helps you handle your dungeon; it never makes your creatures stronger.
