# The Dungeon and Its Rooms

> [!NOTE]
> These are planned features. They are not in the game yet, and details may change before they arrive.

## What it is for

Once every room is built and every amenity is at its top, there is little left to decide about your dungeon. These
ideas make layout a puzzle, let you bend the dungeon's rules, and turn the Temple and the Workshop into places of
discovery.

## Neighbours

Rooms care what they touch. For example:

- A Torture Chamber sharing a wall with the Prison converts captives faster.
- A Library beside the Temple reads forbidden things sooner, and its Warlocks pray more.
- A Lair touching a Hatchery lets creatures eat without leaving it.
- A Graveyard beside a Lair upsets everyone but Vampires.

Six pairs to begin with, each shown as a line of light along the shared wall. Some pairs pull against each other,
so no plan has everything.

## Unholy unions

Two amenities at their top [mastery](AMENITY-MASTERY.md) can be joined at the Temple into one named union with a
new behaviour. For example:

- **Library and Sight of Evil**: a Scrying Room that always sees around itself.
- **Prison and the Lightning trap**: the Chair, which makes captives talk.
- **Hatchery and Poison Gas**: chickens that poison the heroes who steal them.
- **Lair and Heal**: an Infirmary.

Each room can take one union, chosen from two.

## Heresies

Rare tomes, found only far from home, that rewrite one rule of your dungeon at a price. A dungeon can hold two at
most. For example:

- **Open Graves**: no Lairs are needed; creatures sleep where they stand and heal at half speed.
- **Company Scrip**: no wages, but everyone eats twice as much.
- **One Kind**: your portals bring a single kind you name, five levels up, and nothing else.
- **Night Court**: double work by night, and sleep by day.

A tome can be burned at the Temple to undo it.

## The unholy cookbook

Beyond the sacrifices every keeper knows, each world hides a dozen more three-ingredient recipes with results made
for the endless world: reveal the nearest portal, re-roll the choices a book offers, call tonight's wild pack to
fight for you. Wrong mixes do nothing, or bite. Lore pages found in ruins give away one ingredient each, and a
cookbook page keeps what you have learned. The recipes differ from world to world, so on a server knowledge spreads
by talk.

## Rigging

The Workshop builds trigger plates and links them to your other devices:

- A plate, or an Alarm trap, can lock a door or fire a trap somewhere else.
- A door can be set to lock once a number of heroes have passed through it.
- A hero killed by a chain of three devices drops twice his purse.

Rigging needs a new item, the trigger plate, with its own art.
