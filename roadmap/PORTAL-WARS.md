# Portal Wars

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A war that two keepers arrange between themselves, for a stake, at an hour they agree.

1. **The challenge.** A keeper challenges another and puts up a stake. It can be gold, a book or a prisoner with a
   name.
2. **The answer.** The challenged keeper is free to refuse, at no cost. If they accept, they choose the hour.
3. **A day to prepare.** The attack will come through the defender's own portal, and the defender gets a day to
   build walls round it, lay traps and post guards.
4. **The attack.** When the hour comes, twelve of the attacker's creatures step out of that portal.
5. **The result.** The attackers have to take the flame from the defender's Dungeon Heart and carry it back into the
   portal. If they manage it, the attacker has won. If they are all killed, or dawn comes first, the defender has.

The stake goes to the winner. Nothing else is lost, and no Heart is destroyed.

- **A fair fight.** The attacking party's levels are capped at two above the defender's best creature.
- **No easy targets.** No dungeon can be challenged in its first seven days.
