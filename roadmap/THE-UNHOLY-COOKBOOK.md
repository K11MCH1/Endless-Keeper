# The Unholy Cookbook

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Beyond the sacrifices every keeper knows, each world hides a dozen more three-ingredient recipes with results made
for the endless world: reveal the nearest portal, re-roll the choices a book offers, call tonight's wild pack to
fight for you. Wrong mixes do nothing, or bite. Lore pages found in ruins give away one ingredient each, and a
cookbook page keeps what you have learned. The recipes differ from world to world, so on a server knowledge spreads
by talk.
