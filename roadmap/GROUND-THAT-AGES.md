# Ground That Ages

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Today the parts of the world that nobody is near are frozen, and they are exactly as you left them when you come
back. With this, time passes there too. When you return to a place, the game works out what the days you were away
have done to it. For example:

- An outpost you cleared may have a few heroes in it again, or may have fallen into ruin.
- Bodies have turned to bones.
- Burrows have filled in.
- A rival keeper has built another room.
- In [The Overgrowth](THE-OVERGROWTH.md), the earth has closed.

Two things are kept safe:

- **A keeper's own land never ages**, and neither does a co-op dungeon.
- **A cleared stronghold gives notice.** The map tells you how much longer a stronghold you cleared will stay that
  way.
