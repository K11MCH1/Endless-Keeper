# The Overgrowth

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A new biome in which dug earth does not stay dug. Each dawn, the earth creeps back over any tunnel or cave floor
that has no owner, slab by slab, starting at the edges.

- **Claim as you go.** Claimed ground stays open for good. A party that leaves its tunnel unclaimed will find the
  way home closed behind it.
- **Strongholds are reached afresh.** Every visit to a stronghold in the Overgrowth means digging a new way to it.
- **Nothing is buried alive.** The earth never closes on a slab that something is standing on.
- **Its own residents.** The heroes of the Overgrowth are Druids, and its wild creatures are the Maiden of the Nest
  with her Spiderlings.

The Overgrowth changes the ground itself, so it arrives in newly created worlds; worlds that already exist keep
their ground.
