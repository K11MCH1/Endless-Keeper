# Marching Order

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game.

A party formed in the Barracks gets four posts, and each post gives something to whoever holds it:

- **Van**: takes slightly fewer hits.
- **Flank**: is quicker on its feet, and gets its blow in first.
- **Rear**: shoots from further away.
- **Standard**: the creature that carries the standard gives the whole party one gift, and its kind decides which.
  With an Orc the party hits harder, with a Warlock its spells come back sooner, and with a Dark Mistress its
  wounded move faster.

A post gives its bonus wherever the creature stands in a fight. Nobody is asked to hold a line.

So a party is put together, and not only gathered: Warlocks in the Rear behind Bile Demons in the Van, and a Dark
Mistress on the Flank.
