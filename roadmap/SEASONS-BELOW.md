# Seasons Below

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The underworld gets a year: twenty-eight days in four seasons of seven days each. It is the same year for the whole
world, and it is shown as a ring around the sun and moon dial.

- **The Thaw.** Hatcheries produce chickens a quarter faster, and every lake is thick with Flies.
- **The Swelter.** The heroes send one more raid each day, and fire does more harm.
- **The Rot.** Hatcheries produce half as many chickens, and in the Graveyard each body counts as two.
- **The Long Night.** Night lasts twelve minutes and day only eight, the other way round from the rest of the year.
  Wild packs are bigger, and portals take longer to bring creatures.

The fat seasons are for getting ready. The Swelter is the time to stock the Treasury and the Hatchery, since the Rot
follows it.

A new dungeon is spared the worst. For its first seven days it feels only the kinder half of each season.
