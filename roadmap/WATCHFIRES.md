# Watchfires

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A watchfire is a brazier that your Workshop builds and an imp carries out. It can be set down anywhere you have
already seen, and it burns for one day, at the price of a little gold.

Within its light:

- Wild packs cannot appear, and no burrow can open.
- Creatures of yours that are far from home do not lose their nerve.
- The ground stays visible on your map.

A watchfire does not keep the night away. It chooses where the night arrives: a pack that the light keeps out comes
up at its edge instead, and bigger.

Fires are few. Each Workshop keeps only so many burning, and every one of them costs gold each night. A creature
with the vice Afraid of Nothing in the Dark (see [Misdeeds](MISDEEDS.md)) needs no fire.
