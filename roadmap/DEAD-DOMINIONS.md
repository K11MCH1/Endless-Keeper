# Dead Dominions

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Some of the world's ruins were never the heroes'. They are what is left of a keeper who died long ago:

- **Rooms**, broken but still there: a Temple missing half its tiles, a Workshop buried in rubble, a Graveyard with
  no room left in it.
- **Traps** that nobody owns, still armed.
- **A cracked pedestal**, empty, where a Dungeon Heart once beat.

The rooms can be claimed, and a claimed room works, whether or not you have ever learnt to build it. Building more
of it still takes the book.

The farther from home a dead dominion lies, the better its rooms, in the same way as books get better with distance.
