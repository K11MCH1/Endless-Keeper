# World Temperaments

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

A world can be dealt a temperament: three traits and one wonder, shown beside its name in the World List. For
example: "Drowned, long-nighted, poor in gold; holds the Lake of Fire".

- **Traits** push the world one way or another when it is made: its mix of biomes, how high the lava and the water
  stand, how much gold it has, how thickly its strongholds stand, and how long its days and nights are. Every trait
  that makes a world harder is balanced by something that makes it kinder, so that no world gets only hardship.
- **The wonder** is a single place, found once in that world. It gives one lasting gift to the keeper who holds it.

Temperaments are optional, and the choice is made once: a switch offered when a world is first created. A
temperament is never given to a world that already exists, and once dealt it cannot be changed.

Temperaments change the ground itself, so they arrive in newly created worlds; worlds that already exist keep their
ground.
