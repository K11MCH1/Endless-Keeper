# The Auction House

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Keepers on the same server sell to each other and buy from each other. Four kinds of goods can be offered:

- **Creatures** of your own.
- **Heroes** you hold captive.
- **Spellbooks.**
- **Special boxes.**

You put goods up for sale at a stall next to your portal. Any keeper on the server can buy them, for gold, and the
portals deliver what was bought.

A keeper with a Torture Chamber and no Knight will pay well for a captured one, which gives the Prison something to
export.

Prices are kept within limits, and a keeper can buy only so much in a day, so that the Auction House cannot be used
to pour one dungeon's gold into another.
