# Strays

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The wild creatures of the night can be tamed, with chickens.

- **The first chicken.** Give a wild creature a chicken at night: toss one to a Spider, for example. It eats, and at
  dawn it does not fade away with the rest of its pack. It stays where you fed it, as a neutral creature.
- **After three nights of feeding** it is ready to come home. The next chicken you hold out leads it back to your
  dungeon, where it makes a lair, provided you have room for one more creature.
- **It joins at half its level.** A newcomer from a portal starts at level 1 and a wild pack is close to your best
  creature, so a stray keeps half the level it had in the wild.
- **Any kind.** Taming is not limited to the kinds your portals bring.

Zombies are the exception: the only way to own one is still through the Prison and the Torture Chamber, as
[New Creatures](NEW-CREATURES.md) describes.
