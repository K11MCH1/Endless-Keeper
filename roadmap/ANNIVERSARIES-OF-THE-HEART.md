# Anniversaries of the Heart

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Each dungeon counts its own days survived. Days 7, 30, 100 and 365 are marked with a gift (a special box), a change
to the flame of your Heart, and a line in the chat for everyone to see. Lose the Heart and its age is lost with it.
