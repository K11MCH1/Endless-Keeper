# Borderlands

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The strip of land between two biomes gets a name and a rule of its own. For example:

- **Steam country**, between the Volcanic Rifts and the Frozen Hollows. Steam from its vents blots out your map for
  a few seconds, again and again.
- **Oasis**, between the Flooded Grottos and the Sandstone Wastes. No ground in the world suits a Hatchery better,
  and it always belongs to somebody already.
- **Vault country**, between the Crystal Caverns and the Ancient Ruins. It holds twice as many caves as other land,
  all of them sealed.
- **Obsidian**, between the Rocky Depths and the Volcanic Rifts. Its rock can be dug, though ten times more slowly
  than usual.

Six borders are named to begin with. A border is at least a dozen slabs wide, and the map names it.

Borderlands change the ground itself, so they arrive in newly created worlds; worlds that already exist keep their
ground.
