# The Keeper's Champion

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

One of your creatures can be made your Champion, at the Dungeon Heart. It is as close as a keeper comes to walking
the dungeon in person.

- **One key possesses it.** A single key puts you inside your Champion, wherever it is.
- **Your levels are its levels.** Your player level is added to its own. Like [The Hand Grows](THE-HAND-GROWS.md),
  it is another way for player levels to pay out.
- **It can't be killed for good.** A fallen Champion is pulled back to the Heart, where it lies out of action for a
  whole day and wakes one level lower.
- **One change a week.** You can pass the title to another creature once a week.

Two limits keep it fair. The added levels never count as your best creature level, so they make neither the raids on
you nor the [circles](CIRCLES-OF-HELL.md) round you any stronger. And they are capped at your dungeon's age in days,
so a keeper who starts again does not begin with a giant.
