# Foul Chemistry

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Attacks begin to react with each other, whether they come from a creature, a trap or a spell. For example:

- **Gas and fire.** A cloud from a Bile Demon or a Poison Gas trap catches fire if a Lava trap, a Hell Hound or a
  Dragon sets it alight.
- **Ice and boulders.** A Boulder that hits a frozen enemy shatters it, and does twice the damage.
- **Lightning and water.** A bolt that hits one creature standing in water hits every creature standing in that
  water.
- **Fire and ice.** A frozen creature thaws when fire touches it.

There are six pairings to begin with, and each has an effect of its own to show that it happened. Parties and trap
rooms can be built round one: gas and Dragons, or a moat and Dark Mistresses.

The same goes for heroes: fire from a Wizard will set off the gas in your own corridor.
