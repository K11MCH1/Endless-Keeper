# The Keeper's Progression

> [!NOTE]
> These are planned features. They are not in the game yet, and details may change before they arrive.

## What it is for

Your creatures grow, but you, the keeper, grow very little. These ideas give the keeper a record, rewards you feel
every minute, a reason to celebrate surviving, and a way to carry one favourite through a defeat.

## The Keeper's Rap Sheet

The keeper gets a record like their creatures' [Rap Sheet](MISDEEDS.md): a page of tracked feats written in the
game's voice, each in five tiers, each tier paying player experience. For example:

- **Poultry in motion**: heroes turned into chickens.
- **Bone idle**: Skeletons raised in the Prison.
- **Frequent flyer**: creatures dropped into battle.
- **Night shift**: an outpost cleared between dusk and dawn.
- **Clean hands**: a raid beaten without casting a spell.

Your top three feats show beside your name in the player panel.

## The Hand grows

Player levels pay out on the Hand of Evil itself:

- **A bigger grip.** The Hand holds more creatures as you level: nine, then ten, and on up to sixteen.
- **A wider slap.** A slap also hurries the creatures standing next to the one you slap.

The Hand helps you handle your dungeon; it never makes your creatures stronger.

## Anniversaries of the Heart

Each dungeon counts its own days survived. Days 7, 30, 100 and 365 are marked with a gift (a special box), a change
to the flame of your Heart, and a line in the chat for everyone to see. Lose the Heart and its age is lost with it.

## The last loyalist

When your dungeon falls, before you start again, choose one survivor, or the body of your greatest veteran, to walk
with you. It arrives at your new Heart with its name and its record, at half its level, and rests until its first
fight. It doesn't count towards the strength of the raids that come for you until it has fought.
