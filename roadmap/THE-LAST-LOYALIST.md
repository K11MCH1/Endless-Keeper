# The Last Loyalist

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

When your dungeon falls, before you start again, choose one survivor, or the body of your greatest veteran, to walk
with you. It arrives at your new Heart with its name and its record, at half its level, and rests until its first
fight. It doesn't count towards the strength of the raids that come for you until it has fought.

## Still being decided
