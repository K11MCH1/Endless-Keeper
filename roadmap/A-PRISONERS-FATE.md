# A Prisoner's Fate

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

Each captive gets a real choice instead of only "convert or starve":

- **Ransom**: gold now, and the hero comes back in a later raid a level higher.
- **Interrogate**: it tells you what it knows, such as its stronghold's hidden traps and portal rooms, or the
  nearest place you have not found yet.
- **Convert**, **starve into a Skeleton**, or **sacrifice**, as today.

Interrogation is always worth less than converting a Knight.
