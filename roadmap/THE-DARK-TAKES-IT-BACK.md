# The Dark Takes It Back

> [!NOTE]
> In the game since Endless Keeper 1.4.0-10 Alpha. This page is the design it was built from, and some details
> differ in the game.

Claimed land needs an anchor: the Dungeon Heart, a portal you have claimed, a Treasury, a Guard Post or a lit
[watchfire](WATCHFIRES.md). Each anchor holds the land within its reach. Floor that no anchor reaches is on its own:
if three days pass and no imp has been to it, it turns back into bare path, and the walls beside it are no longer
reinforced.

So a long corridor to nowhere is a choice: look after it, or lose it.

- **You are warned.** The reach of each anchor is drawn on the map, and a slab shows a day ahead that it is about to
  go.
- **Nothing is lost while you are away.** Your caretaker keeps its imps claiming and reinforcing.
- **A co-op dungeon that is frozen does not age either.**
