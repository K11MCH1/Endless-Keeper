# Amenity Mastery

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

## What it is for

A keeper's rooms, spells and traps are the dungeon's amenities. Today a duplicate book lets a room or spell be
researched to its next level straight away, so a couple of duplicates take it to the top and every book after
that is wasted.

Amenity Mastery replaces that. Each amenity is mastered over time, with experience from the duplicates you
bring home, and the best duplicates are found a long way from it.

## How it works

- **Duplicates give experience.** A duplicate book gives experience to its own room or spell, and a duplicate
  workshop box to its own trap.
- **Duplicates are the only source.** Using a room, casting a spell or a trap going off gives nothing. Mastery
  is earned by going out, not by waiting at home.
- **Deeper is worth more.** A duplicate is worth more the deeper the
  [Circle of Hell](CIRCLES-OF-HELL.md) it was found in. One from the far circles is worth many from your own
  doorstep.
- **Every amenity has its own bar.** Rooms, spells and traps each need a different amount, and each level needs
  more than the last. It is meant to take a while.
- **The Library still does the reading.** A full bar doesn't raise the level by itself. It lets your Library
  research that level, so your researchers stay busy.
- **Trap levels are built in the Workshop**, not researched in the Library.
- **Each level costs a little more** to research or build than the one before it.
- **At the top, the book is spent.** A duplicate for something already at its highest level disappears when
  used.

## What a level gives

More rooms get levels than have them today, and traps get levels for the first time. Some spells and traps
go further than others, and the last levels of a few traps change how the trap works rather than only making
it stronger.

What each level does, and how much it takes to get there, is not written here. You find out by earning it.
