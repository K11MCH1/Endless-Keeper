# Attunement

> [!NOTE]
> This is a planned feature. It is not in the game yet, and details may change before it arrives.

The keeper's powers get a skill tree. You spend points along its branches, and the branches you choose decide what
kind of caster you become, so two keepers with the same spells no longer cast alike. For example:

- One branch is for **keeping an army alive**, with powers such as Heal, Protect Monster and Speed Monster.
- One is for **fighting at home**, with powers such as Freeze, Slow and Lightning Strike.
- One is for **leading**, with powers such as Possession, Must Obey and Call to Arms.

It is a build for the keeper, as [vices](MISDEEDS.md) are a build for a creature.
