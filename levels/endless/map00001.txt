REM Endless Mode (main menu > Endless Mode). This map is only a stub: the engine replaces it with the
REM world generated from the seed. New Run gives ENDLESS_WORLD a fresh random seed; the 12345 below
REM is used only when this level is started some other way.
LEVEL_VERSION(1)
SET_GENERATE_SPEED(500)

REM Endless world: the engine generates the world from the seed itself, chunk by chunk, with no
REM edge. Enemy keepers appear as you explore and stay
REM passive until you come within their threat radius. There is no win; you lose with your heart.
ENDLESS_WORLD(12345)
REM Research-book progression: you start with the Treasury, Lair,
REM Hatchery, Summon Imp and Possession, and learn everything else from research books found in the world.
ENDLESS_PROGRESSION(1)
REM Creatures and heroes can reach level 100.
SET_GAME_RULE(MaxCreatureLevel,100)
